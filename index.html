What changes?

Binary model: multiply by the land mask. A water cell becomes 0 (fails the final yes/no decision).

Index model: calculate the weighted sum first, then mask water as excluded / NoData. Water is not treated as a low suitability score that can be traded off against good terrain.

Try adding water to a high-scoring cell. Its score remains visible before the mask, but disappears from the final candidate map.
