How Might a Wildfire Spread?

Explore a hypothetical ignition near Sooke Lake Reservoir. Change the conditions, watch the fire move through time, and explain the processes and interactions you observe.

Process modelIgnition → combustion → spread
First, discuss: What data would you need to model a wildfire here? Which inputs describe ignition, which shape spread, and which limit it?
IgnitionLightning or a human-caused spark starts the event. The exact location and time here are hypothetical.
Spread interactionsWind direction, upslope movement, vegetation fuel, canopy continuity, and dryness alter spread at every step.
Physical settingSooke Lake water blocks spread. Terrain and the reservoir shoreline use public geographic data.

Data sources and model limits

GEOGRAPHIC DATA

Terrain and lake

Elevation point samples from Natural Resources Canada's CDEM; slope is estimated from this coarse classroom grid. Sooke Lake Reservoir shoreline comes from the CRD water bodies layer.

ILLUSTRATIVE SCENARIO

Weather, fuel, ignition

Wind, fuel dryness, vegetation cover, canopy height, and the lightning ignition are constructed for this activity. They are not observations or measured fire behaviour.

MODEL LIMIT

Think beyond the cells

This simplified cellular simulation omits suppression, spotting, variable wind, detailed fuel moisture, and fire intensity. It must not guide real-world decisions.
